Vanta 1.0.6 includes the new white V logo throughout the app, installer, Windows icons, and website previews.

- Existing installations can use **Settings → Check for updates** to download, verify, install, and reopen the new version.
- Clicking controls and saved profiles keep their existing behavior.
- The source now includes a separate Microsoft Store build and MSIX packaging instructions. Store publication and certification are still pending.

**Downloads:** Choose `Vanta.Auto.Clicker.Setup.exe` for a normal installation, or the portable EXE/ZIP. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for all three.

The direct EXE downloads are still unsigned and may be blocked by Smart App Control or receive SmartScreen warnings. This release does not yet provide a Microsoft Store-signed download. Keep Windows security protections enabled.
